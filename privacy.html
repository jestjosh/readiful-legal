Readiful Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Readiful turns data from Apple Health into daily Readiness, Recovery, Exertion and Sleep scores. This policy explains what Readiful uses, where it's kept, and the choices you have.

Apple Health data

With your permission, Readiful reads heart rate, heart rate variability, sleep, respiratory rate, temperature, VO2 max, workouts, activity and your date of birth from Apple Health. It uses this data only to calculate your scores and show your trends. Readiful never writes to Apple Health.

Raw Health data stays on your iPhone. It is never sold, never shared with advertisers or data brokers, never used for advertising or marketing, and never stored in iCloud.

Your account (optional)

You can use Readiful without an account. If you sign in with Apple or Google, Readiful stores the following in its database (hosted by Supabase) so you can restore it on a new iPhone:

  • your email address and an account ID,
  • your daily scores and short summaries (for example, hours asleep),
  • your settings and journal entries.

Raw Health samples are never uploaded.

Whoop and Oura (optional)

If you connect a Whoop or Oura account directly, Readiful's server stores only the connection and its sign-in tokens. Your Whoop or Oura data goes from their servers straight to your iPhone and is not stored by Readiful.

Ask Readiful

Ask Readiful answers questions using Apple Intelligence on your iPhone. Your questions and data are not sent to Readiful or any other AI provider.

Subscriptions

Readiful Plus is sold through the App Store. Apple handles payment; Readiful never sees your payment details. Readiful uses RevenueCat to keep track of subscription status and purchase history, linked to an anonymous ID or your account ID.

Tracking

Readiful does not track you across other apps or websites and contains no advertising.

Deleting your data

Delete your account any time in Readiful → You → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and everything backed up to it. To stop Readiful reading Health data, open the Health app → your profile → Apps → Readiful. Deleting the app removes everything stored on your iPhone.

Children

Readiful is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.

Readiful Support

Readiful turns your Apple Health data into daily Readiness, Recovery, Exertion and Sleep scores.

Questions or problems: support@readiful.app

My scores didn't update

Readiful reads from Apple Health. If you wore a Whoop, Oura Ring or other wearable, open its app on your iPhone so it syncs to Health, then pull to refresh on Today.

Change Health permissions

Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Readiful.

Manage or cancel Readiful Plus

Settings → your name → Subscriptions, or You → Manage subscription in the app. To restore a purchase on a new device, tap Restore Purchases on the paywall or in You.

Delete your account

In the app: You → Delete account. This removes your account and your backed-up scores. It doesn't cancel a subscription.
